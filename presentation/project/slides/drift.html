NetSentinel · DataNova 2026 · 9 · macro-F1 on CIC-IDS2017, train on the past, test on the future

Attack patterns change, so NetSentinel watches for drift. Trained on Monday to Thursday morning, the model scores 0.998 on that period, but only 0.182 on the following days, when new attack types appear. The drift monitor noticed this without any labels: the alerted traffic shifted, the mix of predictions shifted, and unknown anomalies rose from 0.9 to 5.4 percent. After retraining on just ten percent of the new flows, labelled, the score recovers to 0.925.
