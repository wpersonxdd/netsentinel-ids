Here is the pipeline. Network traffic arrives as flow records, one row per connection. They are cleaned and audited: duplicates, broken rows and columns that would leak the answer are removed. Then two detectors work side by side: a rebalanced ensemble for known attack types, and a novelty detector for unfamiliar ones. Their alerts are grouped into incidents with a priority, a reason and a first response. The analyst confirms or rejects each incident, and the model retrains on those decisions. One command runs everything and writes a full evaluation report.
