On 121 thousand test flows, 99.9 percent of attack flows raise an alert, and only 0.1 percent of normal flows raise a false alarm. Macro F1, which weights every attack family equally, rises from 0.766 without rebalancing to 0.973. But here is the honest reading: most of that jump comes from nine test flows of the two rarest families, Heartbleed and Infiltration, which the unbalanced model missed completely. On the families with enough test data, rebalancing adds a small but statistically significant gain.
