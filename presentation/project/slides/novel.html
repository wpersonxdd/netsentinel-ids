How do we test attacks the model has never seen? Each attack family is hidden from training in turn, and we check how much of it still raises an alert. The classifier alone catches almost none of a hidden family. With the novelty detector, which only knows what normal traffic looks like, web attacks go from 3 to 87 percent and DoS from 3 to 71 percent. On average, 5 percent becomes 33 percent, for about one percent extra false alarms. Port scans, brute force and botnet traffic look normal at the flow level, so they stay hidden. That is a real limit, and I state it openly.
