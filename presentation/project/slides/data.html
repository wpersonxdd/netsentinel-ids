To test on real traffic I used CIC-IDS2017 from the Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity: 2.8 million network flows recorded over five working days, with nine attack families. The imbalance is extreme: forty-five thousand to one between the largest and the smallest family. Real data is also messy. The pipeline repairs it automatically: afternoon timestamps written on a twelve-hour clock without A M or P M, almost three hundred thousand empty rows, and three hundred thousand duplicate flows.
