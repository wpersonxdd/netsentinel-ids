Now the most important slide. Most projects report a random train test split, and so did I: 0.973. When whole attack sessions are kept on one side, the score drops to 0.888, because the random split was leaking similar flows into training. And when I train on the past and test on the future, the score falls to 0.18. Every attack in this dataset happens on a single day, so a model trained on Monday to Thursday has never seen Friday's DDoS, port scans or botnet. A supervised detector only knows yesterday's attacks. That is exactly why NetSentinel adds a novelty detector and fast retraining.
