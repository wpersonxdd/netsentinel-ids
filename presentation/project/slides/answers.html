THE APPROACH

Four hard parts, four answers

Attacks are rare

Rebalanced training shows rare attacks more often (SMOTE).

New attacks appear

A second detector learns only normal traffic and flags the unfamiliar.

Tactics change

A drift monitor notices the shift without labels and triggers retraining.

Analysts drown in alerts

Alerts become explained incidents; analyst decisions retrain the model.

NetSentinel · DataNova 2026 · 3

NetSentinel treats this as four connected problems. Attacks are rare, so training is rebalanced to show rare attacks more often. New attack types appear, so a second detector learns only what normal traffic looks like and flags anything unfamiliar. Tactics change, so a drift monitor notices the shift without needing labels. And analysts drown in alerts, so alerts become explained incidents, and every analyst decision is used to retrain the model.
