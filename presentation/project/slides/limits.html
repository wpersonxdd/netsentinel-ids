NetSentinel · DataNova 2026 · 13

NetSentinel has clear limits. Port scans and botnet traffic look normal at the flow level. The novelty detector costs about one percent extra false alarms, which matters on a large network. And CIC-IDS2017 is from 2017, with known label noise. The next steps follow from that: pair NetSentinel with payload inspection tools such as Zeek or Suricata, tune the alert budget to the size of the security team, and re-run everything on the official DataNova data, which takes one command.
